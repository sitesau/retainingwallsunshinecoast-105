Retaining Walls Sunshine Coast Comparison Explained

A wall on the Blackall Range at Maleny is a different proposition from one on canal fill at Maroochydore. The published guide names the local ground conditions that change the brief, including basalt clay in the range, volcanic soil around Buderim, sandstone near Landsborough and coastal dune sand. Those differences shape what can be built and what must be checked before pricing means much.

Wall types and their site fit

Concrete sleepers are presented as the workhorse option for replacement work and cut-and-fill blocks. Timber sleepers still suit low garden falls and terraces. Core-filled besser block is positioned for taller walls and rendered finishes where the brief is more structural. Rock and boulder walls are described for acreage sites with room to batter back into the slope. Gabion basket walls are framed as a better fit where scour, runoff or erodible ground shape the problem.

Comparing costs on wall-face area

Pricing is square metres of wall face, height multiplied by length, not lineal metres alone. The guide's own example is a wall 1.2 metres high and 10 metres long, which equals 12 square metres. That single calculation explains why two walls with the same length can produce very different prices. The indicative bands are about $150 to $300 per square metre for rock and boulder, $300 to $450 for timber sleepers, $250 to $430 for gabion, $550 to $700 for concrete sleepers and $550 to $750 for core-filled block. The exclusion list is as important as the rate: GST, excavation, drainage, imported backfill, engineering and certification.

Approval and certification checks

Sunshine Coast Council requires a building permit before construction, modification or repair of most retaining walls. Council approval as a concurrence agency is required for walls over 2 metres. For taller walls, or walls carrying a surcharge, the engineered pathway uses AS 4678 design with Form 15 and Form 16 certification. If there is a driveway, structure or other load above the wall, ask for that pathway to be spelled out in writing before excavation starts.

Who this applies to

This guide is most useful when the site has a fall that limits how the land can be used, when an older wall is bulging or cracked, or when drainage may be the hidden cause behind visible movement. It is also useful when the owner is hearing different answers from a landscaper, mason, bricklayer or excavator operator and needs a cleaner way to frame the job. QBCC licensing is triggered by the value of the building work, not the height of the wall, and structural retaining walls are Class 10b structures designed to AS 4678.

  1. Measure the wall face. Multiply height by length so every quote is compared on square metres of wall face.
  2. Mark any surcharge. Note any driveway, structure or other load above the wall before asking for a final recommendation.
  3. Record ground type. Describe the ground as best you can, matching it to basalt clay, volcanic soil, sandstone or coastal dune sand.
  4. Ask for the drainage detail. State the subsoil drainage behind the wall rather than assuming it is included.

Common questions

Will this wall need a building permit? Sunshine Coast Council requires a building permit before construction, modification or repair of most retaining walls, and concurrence agency approval for walls over 2 metres.

Does height or surcharge trigger AS 4678 design? Yes, walls over 1 metre or walls carrying a surcharge point to an engineered pathway with AS 4678 design and Form 15 and Form 16 certification.

Are Form 15 and Form 16 included in the scope? Check whether engineering and certification are included in the quote, since the published rate bands exclude GST, excavation, drainage, imported backfill, engineering and certification.
